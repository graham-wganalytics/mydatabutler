Who We Are

Your information custodians

With the rapid pace of change in technology, we're generating more data than ever. That's great, but without order, data just becomes noise. My Data Butler works with your team to throw out the garbage and make sure your information systems are clean.


Everybody wants automation, insightful reports, and real-time information to make decisions. But without sanitized data, none of those systems will work right. My Data Butler can come in, clean up the mess, and put a system in place to make sure your data stays right moving forward.
